# V1 planning BOM baseline

**Owner:** [GHO-26](https://linear.app/ghostnet-labs/issue/GHO-26), complete and baseline the V1 BOM.  
**Status:** Planning baseline compiled 2026-10-09. This is not a released manufacturing BOM and freezes nothing. It does not create or change any decision.

This file puts the Track B line items in one table, with ordering, lifecycle and source data next to each one. It owns none of the facts it lists:

- Selections and their status belong to the B-register in [v1-selections.md](v1-selections.md).
- Decisions belong to [../decisions.md](../decisions.md).
- Calculated values belong to the hardware records named in the Source column.

If this file disagrees with one of those records, the record wins. The disagreement then goes in [Conflicts](#conflicts) and to a Linear issue (README rule 6), not into a silent fix here. Rows with no B-ID are parts the hardware records name but the B-register does not list yet.

## How to read the table

- **Status** uses the project's words ([../README.md](../README.md#status-words)). Selected means chosen but not frozen. Candidate means the leading option but not chosen. TBD means open.
- **Manufacturer and MPN.** Where the register gives only a base part number, the orderable code found at the manufacturer or at DigiKey is shown in brackets. A bracketed code is a lookup result, not a selection.
- **Distributor, price, stock.** Fetched 2026-10-09 from DigiKey's catalog through its `punchouttest.digikey.com` host, because the public storefront and Mouser block automated access. The links point to the public `www.digikey.com` page for the same product ID. Prices are USD per unit at the smallest price break, with the minimum order quantity (MOQ) when it is above 1. Many parts showed "In-Stock: 0", including common TI parts that are normally stocked, so **treat every stock figure as unconfirmed** and recheck on the live storefront before ordering. Mouser was not reachable.
- **Lifecycle** is DigiKey's "Part Status" field, plus the TI product-page status for TI parts. Both were checked 2026-10-09.
- **Alternate.** "Recorded" means a project record already names the alternate. "Listed" means the alternate is a real catalog part that was found but has not been reviewed for this design. No alternate is selected by this file.

## Lifecycle and availability risks

These figures come from the 2026-10-09 lookups and are not verified orders.

- **No lifecycle flags.** No line item showed NRND, EOL or Obsolete at DigiKey or TI.
- **Stock figures are unreliable.** Most parts showed zero stock on the catalog host (see How to read the table). Recheck every part on the live storefront.
- **Reel-only parts.** TUSB4041IPAPRG4 is sold only as a 1,000-piece reel and is not normally stocked. TE 2199119-6 is sold only as a 1,200-piece reel. Prototype quantities need the tube or cut-tape variant (listed alternate), a broker or a sample request.
- **TPS25751A orderables are not at DigiKey.** Neither TPS25751ADREFR nor TPS25751ASRSMR was found; only the non-A TPS25751 is listed. Prototype supply may have to come from TI direct.
- **Single-source parts.** B-02 (Gateworks), B-03 (AsiaRF) and B-22 (OpenMANET) are each sold only by their maker, with no distributor listing or published lifecycle policy for the part.
- **B-13 latch.** The only DigiKey listing is a Marketplace item with no price and no backorders, under a different MPN suffix (see Conflicts).
- **B-12 contacts.** The DigiKey listing shows no price, with a 4-week lead time.
- **Long lead times** (DigiKey standard lead time): MAX-M10S-00B 18 weeks, TPS386000RGPR 18 weeks, Molex Pico-Lock parts 19 weeks, CM5 connector 16 weeks, LTW feed-through and cap 15 weeks, supercapacitors 15 weeks.
- **Temperature ratings below the thermal estimates.** These are not lifecycle risks, but they could force a part change:
  - The TE 2199119-6 socket is rated -40 to 80 °C at DigiKey. The thermal plan estimates the Wi-Fi card at 87 to 124 °C at 16 W (flat-shell model with a pad, before the D-035 fins are sized).
  - The AW7916-AED's published operating range (-10 to +70 °C) does not cover D-028's -17.8 °C cold endpoint. This is already recorded in the thermal plan.
  - The SCC supercapacitors are rated to 65 °C at 2.7 V. The enclosure interior is estimated at about 60 °C.
